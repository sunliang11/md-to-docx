# Q3 2026 Business Review

**Period:** July 1 – September 30, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Strategy & Operations

## Overview

Q3 delivered strong revenue growth driven by enterprise adoption and improved conversion from trial to paid. Customer retention improved across all segments.

> Revenue exceeded plan by 12%. Net retention reached 118%, the highest quarterly figure this year.

## Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q3 2026 | Change |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|
| ARR | $4.2M | $4.9M | +17% |
| New customers | 142 | 178 | +25% |
| Churn rate | 3.1% | 2.4% | -0.7pp |
| NPS | 42 | 47 | +5 |
| Support tickets / 1K users | 8.2 | 6.5 | -21% |

## Highlights

- Launched self-serve onboarding — reduced time-to-value from 14 days to 3 days
- Signed 3 Fortune 500 pilot agreements
- Shipped API v2 with backward-compatible migration path
- Expanded APAC sales team (Singapore, Tokyo)

## Challenges

- Infrastructure costs grew faster than revenue in August (resolved in September)
- Two enterprise deals slipped to Q4 due to procurement cycles
- Hiring pace for senior engineers below target
